Flopping around Europe together in 2026. We've been playing music together what feels like our entire lives. Always recorded everything ourselves with the gear we had. Our draw will probably be minimal — but we stand out with passion, joyful smiles, and reckless commitment.

We play venues, bars, festivals, private parties, funerals, barmitzvahs — we really don’t care. Seriously.



Our performance includes scratchy guitar dances, sentimental trumpet solos, dreamy piano ballads and groovy drum machine vibes.
